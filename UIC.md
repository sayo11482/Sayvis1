# User Cognitive Model (UIC) Specification

The **User Cognitive Model (UIC)** is the foundational epistemic substrate through which SAYVIS builds an accurate, evolving understanding of the human owner.

## Epistemic Lifecycle States
1. `OBSERVED`: Directly witnessed facts or explicit telemetry signals.
2. `INFERRED`: Derived hypotheses generated by the cognitive reasoning engine. Inferences are never treated as facts without verification.
3. `CONFIRMED`: Explicitly verified and affirmed by the human owner.
4. `EXPIRED`: Time-bounded observations or habits that have lapsed.
5. `REVOKED`: Explicitly disavowed or invalidated by the human owner.

## Provenance Tracking
Every attribute records its origin:
- `USER_EXPLICIT`: Directly provided via chat or UIC editor.
- `SYSTEM_OBSERVED`: Sensor, time, or battery telemetry.
- `AI_INFERRED`: Pattern synthesized by orchestrator.
- `IMPORTED`: Ingested from external notes or documents.
- `DEVICE_SIGNAL`: Multi-device state broadcast.

## Privacy Classification
- `STANDARD`: General operational metadata.
- `SENSITIVE`: Working habits, schedule preferences, productivity metrics.
- `CONFIDENTIAL`: Explicit personal constraints, health rhythms, private long-term goals.
